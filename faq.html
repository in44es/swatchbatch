How to Make Sims 4 CC & FAQ

Everything beginners need to know about how to make Sims 4 CC, easily recolor furniture with Easy4CC tools, and install .package files safely.

How to make Sims 4 CC easily without Blender?
Making custom content for The Sims 4 doesn't require 3D modeling! With Easy4CC, you can make Sims 4 CC in minutes:

• Use SwatchBatch to easily recolor Sims 4 furniture and objects with custom hex colors or fabric patterns.
• Use EaselEase free in your browser to turn personal photos or artwork into playable easel paintings and wall art CC.
How can I easily recolor Sims 4 furniture and CC?
To recolor easy CC in The Sims 4, open your cloned object .package in SwatchBatch, select areas like cushions or wood frames using the magnetic lasso, apply your custom colors or seamless patterns while keeping realistic shading, and export a standalone .package directly for your Mods folder.
How do I install my creations into The Sims 4?
Both SwatchBatch and EaselEase export standard Sims 4 .package files. Simply copy or drag the .package file into your Mods folder:

Documents > Electronic Arts > The Sims 4 > Mods

Make sure to enable custom content in your game options: Game Options > Other > Enable Custom Content and Mods (and check Script Mods Allowed if applicable), then restart the game.
Do I need Blender or 3D modeling skills?
Absolutely not! That's the entire purpose of Easy4CC tools. Traditional modding tutorials often ask players to install Blender, learn 3D UV unwrapping, and configure complex shader nodes just to change a couch color. Our tools let you recolor objects and generate custom paintings directly, without touching any 3D software.
What is the difference between SwatchBatch and EaselEase?
SwatchBatch is an offline desktop app (Windows 64-bit) focused on recoloring existing furniture and objects (couches, beds, chairs, kitchen counters). It features magnetic lasso selection, fabric pattern tiling, and shading preservation.

EaselEase is a 100% free web app that runs right in your browser (no installation). It turns any image or photo into paintings that your Sims can paint live on the easel during gameplay, or into decorative wall art.
Will these creations overwrite existing game items?
No! For SwatchBatch, creations are exported as standalone catalog packages with their own unique catalog thumbnails and color swatches. They sit alongside the original game furniture, so nothing is replaced or lost.

For EaselEase, wall canvases are also standalone decor objects. Easel paintings integrate neatly into the existing easel painting repertoire of your Sims.
Do I need Sims 4 Studio installed?
For SwatchBatch, you only use Sims 4 Studio for 10 seconds initially to clone the object package you wish to recolor (via "Create 3D Mesh"). SwatchBatch handles the rest and exports the final package. It even includes a quick shortcut button to open your export in Sims 4 Studio if you want to inspect it.

For EaselEase, you do not need Sims 4 Studio at all! The package is assembled directly online in your browser.
Are these tools safe to use?
Yes, 100%. No account creation, no trackers, no subscriptions. SwatchBatch operates completely offline on your PC, and EaselEase processes your images directly in your browser.
