Turn personal photos, digital paintings, and family portraits into real The Sims 4 custom content in seconds. Generate paintings that your Sims actually paint at the easel, or ready-to-hang decorative wall canvases. No software to download!

TWO WAYS TO ENJOY IN-GAME

Easel Artworks or Decorative Wall Canvases

Choose how you want your creations to live inside your Sims' homes.

1. Playable Easel Paintings

Make your Sims paint your own chosen artworks! EaselEase creates painting replacements that appear directly on the easel when your Sim chooses to paint in live mode.

  • ✓ Fits game canvas dimensions
  • ✓ Sims autonomously or manually paint your artwork
  • ✓ Can be signed, sold, or framed in-game

2. Wall Art & Posters Decor

Want to decorate immediately without having a Sim paint? Turn any picture into a decorative wall art object available in Build/Buy mode.

  • ✓ Ready-to-hang custom wall frames & posters
  • ✓ Clean catalog entry in Wall Decorations
  • ✓ Custom catalog title & swatches
WORKFLOW

Three simple steps, right in your browser

No account, no plugins. Works immediately on mobile, Mac, or Windows.

STEP 01

Upload your image

Open EaselEase in your browser. Choose any JPEG, PNG, or WebP image from your computer or phone.

STEP 02

Choose your format

Select either a playable easel artwork or decorative wall canvas, and adjust frame style and aspect ratio.

STEP 03

Drop into Mods

Download the generated .package file, drop it into your Documents/Electronic Arts/The Sims 4/Mods folder, and enjoy!
