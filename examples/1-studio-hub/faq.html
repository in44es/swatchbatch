Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Pop Studio tools, installation in The Sims 4, and package safety.

How do I install exported creations into The Sims 4?
Both SwatchBatch and EaselEase export standard Sims 4 .package files. Simply move the downloaded .package into your Mods folder, usually located at:
Documents / Electronic Arts / The Sims 4 / Mods.
Make sure "Enable Custom Content and Mods" is checked in your game options.
Do I need Blender or 3D modeling software?
No! All Pop Studio tools are built specifically so you never have to touch Blender, UV unwrapping, or 3D baking. SwatchBatch works directly on 2D diffuse textures, and EaselEase converts images into paintings automatically.
What is the difference between SwatchBatch and EaselEase?
SwatchBatch is a Windows desktop application to recolor 3D objects (couches, chairs, tables, beds) with magnetic lasso precision, multi-part layering, and pattern tiling.

EaselEase is a 100% free web app that runs in your browser to convert photos and pictures into paintings (playable easel artworks that Sims paint live, or decorative wall canvases).
Are exported items standalone or overrides?
For SwatchBatch: They are true standalone catalog objects with their own new swatches and catalog entries. They do not overwrite original in-game items.

For EaselEase: Wall decor creations are standalone objects. Easel paintings integrate into the in-game easel painting categories so your Sims paint them during gameplay.
Do I need Sims 4 Studio installed?
For SwatchBatch, you only use Sims 4 Studio initially to extract/clone the source package via "Create 3D Mesh". SwatchBatch directly compiles the finished package, with an optional one-click button to inspect your export in Studio.

For EaselEase, you do not need Sims 4 Studio at all — packages are generated online directly.
Are these tools safe and private?
Yes, 100%. No account, no telemetry, no subscription. SwatchBatch runs entirely locally on your Windows computer. EaselEase processes your images in your browser.
