✦ THE RECOLORING WORKFLOW

One object. Your own little collection.

No complex 3D meshes to bake. SwatchBatch automates the technical Sims 4 packaging so you can focus purely on creativity.

STEP 01

Select any supported Sims 4 object .package. SwatchBatch extracts included diffuse textures, catalog text, and swatches automatically.

✦ CRAFTED FOR SIMS 4 CREATORS

Everything you need for clean, pro CC

SwatchBatch replaces tedious photo editing and manual package hex editing with an enjoyable, visual desktop workshop.

Magnetic Lasso Selection

Snap contours effortlessly along edges, folds, and seams. Zoom from 100% up to 1600% with smooth panning for pixel-perfect selections.

Realistic Relief & Shading

Our shading pipeline preserves the original object's shadows, creases, and highlights (adjustable from 0% flat color to 100% full relief). Never get washed out or muddy textures.

Multi-Part Calques & Overlays

Recolor different parts of the same object independently (e.g., wooden frame, leather seat, metallic pins). Each part holds its own selection and palette.

Fabric & Pattern Support (Full)

Import your own PNG/JPG patterns. Adjust tiling scale, stretch, offset, rotation, and mirror. SwatchBatch retains authentic original RGB colors with realistic texture shading.

True Standalone Packages

Generates clean, compliant The Sims 4 DBPF packages with proper unsigned variant-sorted MTST tables and custom localized string tables. Ready to drop in your Mods folder.

Private & Offline By Design

No internet required, no subscriptions, no accounts, and no AI cloud uploads. Your custom content, project files, and assets remain 100% on your machine.

✦ COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SwatchBatch, installation, and game compatibility.

Do I need Blender or 3D knowledge to use SwatchBatch?
No! SwatchBatch is purpose-built to eliminate the need for Blender, texture baking, or complex mesh editing when recoloring. You work directly on 2D diffuse textures with smart magnetic lasso selection and instant shading preview.
Are exported packages standalone CC or recolor overrides?
They are true standalone catalog items. When exported, SwatchBatch generates new internal identifiers (OBJD, COBJ, MTST tables, and private string tables). Your new item appears as a separate catalog object with its own price, title, description, and swatches, leaving the original EA or CC item intact.
Do I need to install Python or Node.js separately?
Not at all. The Windows installers provided on itch.io include a self-contained, pre-compiled processing runtime (SwatchBatchEngine.exe). You simply run the installer and launch SwatchBatch right away.
Do I need Sims 4 Studio installed?
You only use Sims 4 Studio initially to clone the game object via "Create 3D Mesh" and save your source .package. Once you have that package, SwatchBatch does the rest. After exporting, a convenient "Open in Sims 4 Studio" button is available if you want to inspect your finished creation before launching the game.
What is EaselEase and how does it work?
EaselEase is a 100% free web app by In44es that runs directly in any modern browser (no installation needed). It converts any image or photo into custom The Sims 4 paintings: either as decorative wall art objects for Build/Buy mode, or as custom easel paintings that your Sims actually paint during gameplay.
Can my Sims really paint my custom artwork on the easel in-game?
Yes! EaselEase generates custom easel artwork packages. When your Sim practices painting at an easel, your custom pictures will appear on their canvas as their artwork creation.
What are the system requirements?
Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit), standard display, and enough disk space for your custom packages and Mods folder. No dedicated graphics card required for texture processing.
