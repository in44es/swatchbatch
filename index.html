HOW TO MAKE SIMS 4 CC

Create your first Sims 4 CC in 3 simple steps

Whether you want to recolor Sims 4 furniture or make custom wall art, Easy4CC handles the technical packaging for you.

STEP 01

Pick your CC tool

Use SwatchBatch to easily recolor Sims 4 furniture and objects, or open EaselEase in your browser to make custom paintings CC.

STEP 02

Recolor or upload art

Choose custom hex colors, tile seamless fabric patterns with realistic shading, or upload any photo—no Blender or 3D modeling required.

STEP 03

Export .package & play

Download your standalone .package file, drop it into your Documents/Electronic Arts/The Sims 4/Mods folder, and enjoy your new CC in-game.
