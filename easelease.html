TWO SIMPLE WAYS TO PLAY

Easel Artworks or Decorative Wall Canvases

Choose how you want your images to appear inside your Sims' universe.

1. Wall Art & Poster Decor

Want to redecorate a room immediately without having a Sim paint it? Turn any photo, poster, or artwork into a decorative wall art object ready to place from Build/Buy mode, with 23 Base Game-compatible formats to choose from.

  • ✓ 23 Base Game-compatible styles: framed paintings, pinned posters, frameless canvases & more
  • ✓ 8 built-in photo filters to stylize your artwork before exporting
  • ✓ Found cleanly in the Wall Decorations catalog with custom title, description & price

2. Playable Easel Paintings

Make your Sims actually paint your own chosen artworks! When your Sim sits down at the easel to paint (Classic, Pop Art, Realism, etc.), your custom image appears on their canvas as they paint stroke by stroke.

  • ✓ Fits standard game easel canvas aspect ratios
  • ✓ Sims autonomously or manually paint your chosen picture
  • ✓ Once finished, Sims can sign, frame, keep, or sell the painting
HOW IT WORKS

Three simple steps, straight in your browser

No account, no plugins, no complicated settings. Accessible to every player.

STEP 01

Choose your mode & format

Select decorative wall art (23 Base Game-compatible formats including framed paintings, pinned posters, and frameless canvases) or a playable easel artwork.

STEP 02

Upload & style your image

Open EaselEase in your browser, pick any JPEG, PNG, or WebP photo, and customize it with 8 built-in image filters.

STEP 03

Drop into Mods

Download the generated .package file, drop it into your Documents/Electronic Arts/The Sims 4/Mods folder, and enjoy in-game!
