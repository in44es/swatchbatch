TWO SIMPLE WAYS TO PLAY

Easel Artworks or Decorative Wall Canvases

Choose how you want your images to appear inside your Sims' universe.

1. Playable Easel Paintings

Make your Sims actually paint your own chosen artworks! When your Sim sits down at the easel to paint (Classic, Pop Art, Realism, etc.), your custom image appears on their canvas as they paint stroke by stroke.

  • ✓ Fits standard game easel canvas aspect ratios
  • ✓ Sims autonomously or manually paint your chosen picture
  • ✓ Once finished, Sims can sign, frame, keep, or sell the painting

2. Wall Art & Poster Decor

Want to redecorate a room immediately without having a Sim paint it? Turn any photo, poster, or artwork into a decorative wall art object ready to place from Build/Buy mode.

  • ✓ Ready-to-hang framed prints, posters, and modern canvases
  • ✓ Found cleanly in the Wall Decorations catalog
  • ✓ Custom catalog title, description, and price
HOW IT WORKS

Three simple steps, straight in your browser

No account, no plugins, no complicated settings. Accessible to every player.

STEP 01

Upload your image

Open EaselEase in your browser. Pick any JPEG, PNG, or WebP photo from your computer, tablet, or phone.

STEP 02

Choose your format

Select either a playable easel artwork or decorative wall canvas, and adjust frame style and aspect ratio.

STEP 03

Drop into Mods

Download the generated .package file, drop it into your Documents/Electronic Arts/The Sims 4/Mods folder, and enjoy in-game!
