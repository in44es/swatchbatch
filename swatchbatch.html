HOW TO RECOLOR IN 3 SIMPLE STEPS

One object. Your own customized swatches.

No 3D modeling, no UV mapping. SwatchBatch automates the technical Sims 4 packaging under the hood.

STEP 01

Open your object package

Select any supported Sims 4 object .package. SwatchBatch automatically extracts linked diffuse textures, catalog text, and original swatches.

STEP 02

Customize with style

Trace areas using the magnetic lasso, layer separate parts (cushions, legs, accents), pick hex colors, or import seamless fabric patterns.

STEP 03

Export to your game

Set a custom title, description, and price. Export creates a brand new standalone package ready for your Sims 4 Mods folder, with all swatches built in.

FEATURES

Everything you need for clean, pro Sims 4 CC

SwatchBatch replaces tedious photo editing and manual package hex editing with an enjoyable, visual desktop workshop.

Magnetic Lasso Selection

Snap contours effortlessly along edges, folds, and seams. Zoom from 100% up to 1600% with smooth panning for pixel-perfect selections.

Realistic Relief & Shading

Our shading pipeline preserves the original object's shadows, creases, and highlights (0% flat color to 100% full relief). Never get washed out textures.

Multi-Part Calques & Overlays

Recolor different parts of the same object independently (e.g., wooden frame, leather seat, metallic pins). Each part holds its own selection and palette.

Fabric & Pattern Support (Full)

Import your own PNG/JPG patterns. Adjust tiling scale, stretch, offset, rotation, and mirror. Retains authentic original RGB colors with realistic shading.

True Standalone Packages

Generates clean, compliant The Sims 4 DBPF packages with proper unsigned variant-sorted MTST tables and custom localized string tables. Ready for your Mods folder.

100% Offline & Private

No internet required, no subscriptions, no accounts, and no AI cloud uploads. Your custom content, project files, and assets remain locally on your machine.
